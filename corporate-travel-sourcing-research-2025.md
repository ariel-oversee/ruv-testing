## 3. NDC (New Distribution Capability) in Airline Sourcing

### Market Adoption and Status (2025)

- **Adoption Rate:** Over 60% of airlines have adopted NDC to some degree
- **Market Maturity:** NDC is no longer just innovation—it's a necessity for competitive airlines
- **Industry Consensus:** "NDC in 2025 is essential, not optional"

### Recent Developments (2025)

#### NDC FastTrack Initiative (July 2025)
- **Purpose:** Unite airlines, travel agencies, technology providers, and stakeholders
- **Goals:**
  - Identify best practices for NDC implementation
  - Address technical barriers slowing widespread adoption
  - Accelerate industry standardization

**Inaugural Meeting:** Travelport hosted in Dallas, November 13, 2025

#### Corporate Travel Partnership Launch
**Oversee + FCM Travel Partnership:**
- Launched NDC reshopping capabilities for corporate travel
- Streamlines path from EDIFACT to NDC for eligible itineraries
- Provides access to modern retailing and richer airline content
- Improves servicing capabilities and delivers potential savings

### Business Impact for Corporate Travel

**Cost Benefits:**
- 87% of travel managers report NDC connections help save money
- Airlines position NDC as providing cheapest fares (GDS no longer required)
- Reduced distribution costs passed to corporate clients

**Operational Benefits:**

1. **Personalization**
   - Wide range of ancillary services (seat upgrades, baggage, priority boarding)
   - Customized offers based on traveler profiles and preferences
   - Dynamic packaging of flight + services

2. **Real-Time Information**
   - Live seat availability updates
   - Dynamic pricing and promotional offers
   - Instant booking confirmations and changes

3. **Enhanced Content**
   - Rich media (images, videos, detailed descriptions)
   - Branded fares with clear differentiation
   - Unbundled pricing transparency

### Technical Standards
- **Format:** XML-based data exchange
- **Framework:** IATA-developed standards
- **Model:** Offer and Order management processes
- **Distribution:** Channel-agnostic (works across all booking channels)

## 9. Industry Challenges and Considerations

### Technology Adoption Barriers:
- **Integration Complexity:** Connecting legacy systems with modern platforms
- **Change Management:** User adoption and process transformation resistance
- **Data Quality:** Ensuring accuracy and completeness across multiple sources
- **Vendor Fragmentation:** Managing multiple platforms and interfaces

### NDC-Specific Challenges:
- Technical barriers slowing widespread implementation (addressed by FastTrack Initiative)
- Varying levels of airline NDC capability and content richness
- GDS vs. NDC channel management and optimization
- Training requirements for booking tools and processes

### Data and Analytics Considerations:
- Data privacy and security compliance (GDPR, CCPA, etc.)
- Real-time data processing requirements and infrastructure
- Analytics skill gaps in procurement teams
- Balancing automation with human oversight and judgment
